What Is Palm Springs Jebel Ali?

Palm Springs Jebel Ali, also searched as Nakheel Palm Springs or Palm Springs Dubai, is an upcoming residential community in the Jebel Ali area of Dubai. Rather than a tower district, the plan centres on low-rise living: townhouses and standalone villas arranged around landscaped parks, walkable pedestrian pathways, recreational facilities and a central country club.

Preliminary plans describe a California-inspired neighbourhood. Developer renders show lagoon style pools with sandy beach areas, sports courts, sun loungers and a clubhouse with a gym and dining spaces, which gives a clear sense of the resort-style tone Nakheel is aiming for. The development is planned in two phases.

Low-rise townhouses and standalone villas, landscaped parks and a central country club, from one of Dubai's best-known master developers.

Where Is Palm Springs Jebel Ali?

Palm Springs sits in the Jebel Ali area of southwest Dubai, close to Palm Jebel Ali and Sheikh Zayed Road, the main route between Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Some early listings describe the site as falling within the Hessyan First area, though the exact boundary will be confirmed once Nakheel publishes the official masterplan.

This part of Dubai is changing quickly. Nakheel is building Palm Jebel Ali nearby, with construction contracts worth over AED 3.5 billion awarded in April 2026 for 544 villas, and the wider Jebel Ali corridor is one of the emirate's main growth areas.

Palm Springs vs Palm Jebel Ali: What Is the Difference?

The names are similar and both are by Nakheel, so buyers often confuse them. They are separate developments with different settings and price points.

The townhouse collection is the accessible entry point to the community. The 3 bedroom is expected to be a ground plus one storey home of about 2,350 sq ft, with the 4 bedroom at about 2,850 sq ft. The minimum expected plot size is around 2,000 sq ft.

Palm Springs standalone villas

Why Buyers Are Watching Palm Springs Jebel Ali

A masterplan from an established developer

Nakheel is the developer behind Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Islands and Jumeirah Village, and its 2026 programme also includes Dubai Islands and Palm Jebel Ali. For many buyers that track record is the first thing they check.

Low-rise family living in a growth corridor

Townhouses and villas in planned communities have been a consistent draw for families relocating to Dubai, and Jebel Ali is one of the areas seeing the most new infrastructure and development.

Buyer protections on off-plan purchases

In Dubai, off-plan payments are paid into escrow accounts regulated by RERA and the Dubai Land Department, and released to the developer against construction progress.

Golden Visa eligibility

Property worth AED 2 million or more can qualify for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa, and off-plan property from approved developers can qualify once the registered value reaches that level.
