Marsa Al Saadiyat is located on Saadiyat Island, Abu Dhabi, an AED 100 billion masterplan for the island's final phase. Set along an 8km waterfront with 5.6km of beaches, the community centres on Abu Dhabi's largest marina, with space for up to 350 berths for sailing boats and luxury yachts. The masterplan brings together waterfront apartments, luxury villas, private mansions and branded residences alongside a scenic walkway connecting directly to Saadiyat Cultural District, home to the Louvre Abu Dhabi and Zayed National Museum.

Why Marsa Al Saadiyat Is Generating Strong Early Interest

An AED 100 billion vision for Saadiyat Island's final phase

Marsa Al Saadiyat is backed by an AED 100 billion development value, representing one of the most significant waterfront masterplans currently underway in Abu Dhabi. The scale of the investment alone gives buyers confidence in the ambition and delivery of the masterplan.

Abu Dhabi's largest marina

Set along an expansive 8km waterfront, Marsa Al Saadiyat will be home to Abu Dhabi's largest marina, with capacity for up to 350 berths for sailing boats and luxury yachts. This positions the community as a genuine marina lifestyle address rather than a beachfront development with incidental water access.

Direct connection to Saadiyat Cultural District

A scenic walkway will connect Marsa Al Saadiyat directly to Saadiyat Cultural District, placing residents within reach of the Louvre Abu Dhabi and Zayed National Museum. Planned roads, tunnels and an underground Etihad Rail station will further strengthen connections to central Abu Dhabi and the wider UAE.

A full spectrum of luxury residences

The masterplan will offer waterfront apartments, luxury villas, private mansions and branded residences, giving buyers a range of entry points into one of Saadiyat Island's most significant upcoming addresses.

Cross-border opportunity: Marsa Al Saadiyat is located in Abu Dhabi rather than Dubai, and is featured here given the scale of the masterplan and its position on Saadiyat Island. Buyers should be aware this is an Abu Dhabi freehold community rather than a Dubai one when evaluating the investment. Pricing, floor plans and the payment plan have not yet been released.
