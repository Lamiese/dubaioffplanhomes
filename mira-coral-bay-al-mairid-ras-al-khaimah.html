Mira Coral Bay is located in Al Mairid, Ras Al Khaimah, a branded waterfront community launching in September 2026. The development brings together fully furnished apartments and villas set along the Ras Al Khaimah coastline, designed as a turnkey, move-in-ready lifestyle destination where residents step straight into a finished home without the usual post-handover fit-out. Waterfront promenades, a retail walk and resort-style arrival spaces connect the residential clusters to the shoreline.

Why Mira Coral Bay Is Generating Strong Early Interest

Fully branded, fully furnished, 100% turnkey

Every home at Mira Coral Bay is delivered fully furnished and 100% turnkey, removing the cost and delay of furnishing a new property after handover. This positions the community as a ready-to-live-in or ready-to-rent option from day one, appealing equally to end users and investors targeting immediate rental income.

A premium waterfront setting in Al Mairid

Set on the shores of Al Mairid, Mira Coral Bay combines beachfront living with a landscaped waterfront promenade, a retail walk and resort-style arrival courtyards, all designed around walkability and direct access to the water.

Accessible entry point into branded coastal living

With apartments and villas starting from AED 550,000, Mira Coral Bay offers a comparatively accessible entry point into fully branded, fully furnished waterfront living in one of Ras Al Khaimah's fastest-growing coastal districts.
