Hudayriyat Golf Estates is located on Al Hudayriat Island, Abu Dhabi, developed by Modon Properties, the same developer behind the wider 3,000 hectare Hudayriat Island destination. The community is a premium residential address built around sweeping fairways and waterfront surroundings, offering an exclusive collection of 3 to 6 bedroom townhomes, villas and signature golf mansions. Andalusian-inspired architecture is combined with expansive green landscapes and refined family living, positioned just off central Abu Dhabi opposite Al Bateen.

Why Hudayriyat Golf Estates Is Generating Strong Early Interest

Developed by the team behind the island itself

Modon Properties is the developer behind Hudayriyat Island, and for most buyers, that single fact says a great deal about who they are. The island sits just off Abu Dhabi opposite Al Bateen, 3,000 hectares that Modon has spent years turning into the emirate's sports and wellness playground, home to Surf Abu Dhabi and a growing roster of large-scale, lifestyle-driven developments across the UAE.

Genuine championship golf living

Hudayriyat Golf Estates is built directly around an 18-hole golf course surrounded by expansive green open spaces, with select residences offering direct fairway frontage. This is a genuinely rare offering in Abu Dhabi's current market, combining island living with championship golf in a single address.

A luxury property collection built for scale

The development offers a spacious collection of 3 to 6 bedroom townhomes, golf homes, villas and signature golf mansions, catering to a wide range of family sizes and lifestyle needs. This is a low-density, luxury-first community rather than a high-rise apartment development.

An accessible 5% initial down payment

Despite the luxury positioning, Hudayriyat Golf Estates is being offered with a low 5% initial down payment during the launch phase, alongside a buyer-friendly 40/60 payment structure, making entry more accessible relative to the overall price point.

Lifestyle-focused amenities

Residents will have access to wellness facilities, coworking spaces, cycling trails, multi-sports courts and private educational facilities, all woven into a connected, lifestyle-focused master community designed for long-term family living.

Cross-border opportunity: Hudayriyat Golf Estates is located in Abu Dhabi rather than Dubai, and is featured here given the strength of Modon's track record and the calibre of the golf-front offering. Buyers should be aware this is an Abu Dhabi freehold community rather than a Dubai one when evaluating the investment. Living on Al Hudayriat Island: What to Expect

Connectivity

Hudayriyat Golf Estates offers excellent connectivity through Shakhbout Bin Sultan Street and Hudayriyat Bridge, with convenient access to Sheikh Zayed Bin Sultan Road (E10) for travel across Abu Dhabi.

Surrounding Access

The location is 29 minutes from Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM), 31 minutes from the Louvre Museum, 33 minutes from Zayed International Airport and 37 minutes from Disneyland.

Lifestyle

Al Hudayriat Island has been developed by Modon into Abu Dhabi's sports and wellness destination, home to Surf Abu Dhabi and a wide range of leisure, cycling and outdoor facilities beyond the golf estate itself.

Investment Profile

The combination of championship golf frontage, a low 5% launch phase down payment and Modon's track record as the island's own master developer suits investors seeking strong long-term capital appreciation in Abu Dhabi's luxury villa segment.
