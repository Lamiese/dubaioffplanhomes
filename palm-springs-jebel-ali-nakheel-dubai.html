Palm Springs Jebel Ali is Nakheel's new master-planned community of townhouses and villas on the Dubai mainland, set beside Sheikh Zayed Road and minutes from Palm Jebel Ali. Nakheel briefed brokers on the first release in November 2026, confirming prices, the payment plan and an estimated October 2030 handover, and Expressions of Interest (EOIs) are now being taken through registered agents.

The community is planned across a 382 hectare masterplan with more than 30% given over to open space, around 35 km of cycling and jogging tracks, and a landscape of waterways, gardens and green corridors. Homes come in two collections: 3 and 4 bedroom Aliso townhouses and 4 and 5 bedroom Rancho villas.

Why Palm Springs Jebel Ali Is Drawing Strong Interest

Confirmed pricing and an early-entry first release

The first release is a limited collection of villas and townhouses, priced from AED 3.8M. For buyers who have watched Palm Jebel Ali villas reprice since their launch, Palm Springs is the mainland gateway to the same growth corridor at a more accessible entry point.

A community built around three lifestyle destinations

Canyon Park is the green heart of the masterplan, with streams, ponds, sensory gardens and shallow pools. Thousand Palms Country Club brings a sand-beach pool, restaurants, a wellness centre and an event lawn. Silver Spur Racquet Club adds courts, a gym, fitness studios and a lap pool.

Larger plots, lower density

Nakheel is positioning Palm Springs on space: larger plots and lower density than comparable prime villa communities, at a similar price per square foot. Plot sizes for each unit type are listed in the brochure.

Connected to Dubai's southern growth corridor

The community sits next to Sheikh Zayed Road and Al Maktoum Airport Street, with a Metro station planned alongside it as part of the Red Line extension and Etihad Rail's Al Yalayis station close by. Schools, mosques, healthcare and retail are planned within the wider masterplan.

EOI stage: Palm Springs is currently accepting Expressions of Interest, which are submitted on your behalf by a registered agent ahead of unit allocation. Request the brochure or speak to us to understand the EOI deposit, the documents required and how allocation works for the unit type you want.

Frequently Asked Questions About Palm Springs Jebel Ali

Who is the developer of Palm Springs Jebel Ali?+
Palm Springs Jebel Ali is a master-planned community by Nakheel, part of Dubai Holding Real Estate and the developer behind Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Islands and Palm Jebel Ali.
Where is Palm Springs Jebel Ali located?+
It sits on the Dubai mainland in Jebel Ali, next to Sheikh Zayed Road and Al Maktoum Airport Street. Palm Jebel Ali is about 14 minutes away, Bluewaters Island about 20 minutes and Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC) about 23 minutes, with a Metro station planned alongside the community.
How much are the Palm Springs townhouses and villas?+
Confirmed starting prices are AED 3.8M for the 3 bedroom townhouse, AED 4.6M and AED 5.2M for the two 4 bedroom townhouse types, AED 7.0M for the 4 bedroom villa and AED 8.9M for the 5 bedroom villa. Prices vary by plot and position, so request the price list for current availability.
What is the Palm Springs payment plan?+
The plan is 60/40: 10% on booking, 50% in instalments during construction between 2027 and 2029, and 40% on handover, with construction completion estimated for October 2030. The full dated schedule is included in the brochure.
Is Palm Springs Jebel Ali taking EOIs now?+
Yes. Nakheel briefed brokers in November 2026 and Expressions of Interest are now being submitted through registered agents. Get in touch and we will explain the EOI deposit, the documents needed and how unit allocation works.
When is handover?+
Estimated construction completion is October 2030, when the final 40% becomes due.
Does Palm Springs qualify for the UAE Golden Visa?+
Property worth AED 2 million or more can qualify for the 10 year Golden Visa, and every home at Palm Springs starts above that level. Eligibility is confirmed by the authorities at the time of application.
