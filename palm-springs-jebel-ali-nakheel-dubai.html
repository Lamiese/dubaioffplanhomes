Palm Springs Jebel Ali is a new master-planned community from Nakheel, located in the Jebel Ali area of Dubai, close to Palm Jebel Ali and Sheikh Zayed Road. Planned as a low-rise neighbourhood rather than a tower district, it brings together townhouses and standalone villas designed around family living, landscaped surroundings and shared community facilities. The community is part of Nakheel's 2026 launch programme and is currently at the pre-launch stage, with official pricing, floor plans and the payment plan still to be released.

Why Palm Springs Jebel Ali Is Generating Early Interest

A masterplan from one of Dubai's best-known developers

Nakheel is the developer behind Palm Jumeirah, along with communities such as Jumeirah Islands and Jumeirah Village. Its 2026 programme also includes Dubai Islands and Palm Jebel Ali, which places Palm Springs within a much wider pipeline of Nakheel launches across the city. For many buyers, that track record is the first thing they look at before a project is even priced.

Low-rise living built around families

Palm Springs is planned as a neighbourhood of 3 and 4 bedroom townhouses alongside 4 and 5 bedroom standalone villas. That mix gives families a choice between a more accessible entry point and larger, more private homes with greater separation between neighbours, all within a low-density setting.

A California-inspired community concept

Preliminary plans describe a community with landscaped parks, walkable pedestrian pathways, recreational facilities and a central country club. Developer renders show lagoon style pools, shaded lounging areas and a clubhouse with a gym and dining spaces, giving a clear sense of the resort-style tone Nakheel is aiming for.

Two phases, with the first release expected soon

The development is planned in two phases on Nakheel's 2026 launch programme, with the first release expected soon. Unit types, floor plans, prices and the payment plan are released by the developer at launch, which is why early registration matters for anyone who wants to be ready to choose a unit on day one.

A structured payment plan expected

Early indications point to a 60/40 payment structure, with payments spread across construction and handover rather than being front-loaded. Our payment plan calculator lets you model how a plan like this could look against your own budget.

Palm Springs Jebel Ali Townhouses and Villas

The residential offering is expected to cater to different family sizes and budgets, with early price indications running from around AED 3.8 million up to AED 9 million.

Townhouses

The townhouse collection is expected to include 3 and 4 bedroom homes, offering the more accessible entry point into the community. Preliminary specifications suggest a built-up area of roughly 2,350 sq ft for the 3 bedroom (a ground plus one storey layout) and 2,850 sq ft for the 4 bedroom, with a minimum plot size of around 2,000 sq ft and prices starting from approximately AED 3.8 million.

Standalone villas

The standalone villas are expected to offer larger layouts and greater separation between neighbouring homes. Preliminary plans include 4 bedroom villas of around 4,500 sq ft and 5 bedroom residences of around 5,600 sq ft, with early estimates placing these homes between AED 8 million and AED 9 million.

Palm Springs Jebel Ali Payment Plan

Palm Springs Jebel Ali is expected to offer a 60/40 payment plan for its townhouses and villas, spreading payments across construction and handover. Under the proposed structure, 20% would be due as a down payment on booking, followed by 40% in instalments during construction, with the remaining 40% payable on completion.
