Mira Coral Bay, Ras Al Khaimah:
The World's First Multi-Branded Waterfront Community

Dubai Off Plan Homes  ·  10 November 2026  ·  8 min read
Home› Blog› Mira Coral Bay Guide

Mira Coral Bay is a branded waterfront community in Al Mairid, Ras Al Khaimah, billed as the world's first multi-branded waterfront community. Developed by Mira Developments in a joint venture with Marjan, the master developer behind Al Marjan Island, it brings 14 international luxury brands, more than 800 apartments, 101 villas, four five-star resorts, two beach clubs and a yacht club together on one stretch of the Ras Al Khaimah coastline.

It is also one of the most searched new projects in the northern emirates right now. This guide covers what Mira Coral Bay actually is, which brands are involved, where Al Mairid sits, what the homes look like, and the questions worth asking before you buy an apartment or villa here.

What Is Mira Coral Bay?

Most branded residences in the UAE attach one name to one tower. Mira Coral Bay does something different: it puts 14 brands from fashion, design and luxury into a single masterplan, with each building or cluster created and fully furnished by one brand partner. You are not just choosing an apartment or a villa, you are choosing whose design world you want to live in.

The masterplan is by the Italian architecture studio of Marco Casamonti, with coral-inspired facades, car-free streets, landscaped promenades and a contemporary art gallery. Homes are delivered fully furnished and serviced, so there is no fit-out after handover.

Fourteen luxury houses, one shoreline, and every home delivered furnished by the brand whose name is on the building.

Why Ras Al Khaimah, and Why Now

Ras Al Khaimah has moved from a weekend escape to one of the UAE's busiest property markets. Residential sales reached AED 12.4 billion across around 6,600 transactions in 2025, with off-plan accounting for 85% of deals, and gross rental yields averaged 5.3%. Over the past year, apartment values on Al Marjan Island rose 23.1% and in Al Hamra 14.7%, with apartment rents up 14.3%.

Tourism is the engine. The emirate welcomed a record 1.35 million overnight visitors in 2025, and the US$5.1 billion Wynn Al Marjan Island integrated resort is now expected to open in September 2027, with Ras Al Khaimah targeting 3.5 million visitors a year by 2030. Mira Coral Bay is partnered with Marjan, the same master developer behind Al Marjan Island, and is designed around hospitality from the start, which is why it draws interest from both lifestyle buyers and investors looking at short-stay rental.

The 14 Brands Behind Mira Coral Bay

The brand line-up is the headline. Names confirmed publicly include Dolce&Gabbana Casa, Bentley Home, ETRO Home, Jacob & Co., Trussardi and John Richmond, whose Richmond Residences bring his bold, fashion-led aesthetic into the interiors. Mira has also introduced its own branded villa concept, Kadar Villas.

Each brand is matched to a specific building or cluster, and that matters when you buy. Two apartments with the same layout can feel completely different depending on the brand, and resale and rental demand can differ between collections too. Which brand sits where, and which collections are open now, is the first thing we go through with buyers.

Mira Coral Bay Apartments, Townhouses and Villas

Branded apartments

What they are: more than 800 fully furnished residences from studios to 3 bedrooms, with studios starting at around 420 sq ft. Who they suit: buyers wanting an accessible entry into branded waterfront living, from around AED 550,000, and investors focused on rental, since the homes come rental-ready.

Townhouses

What they are: furnished family homes within the brand clusters, giving more space than an apartment with the same resort services. Who they suit: families and second-home buyers who want a lock-up-and-leave home near the beach.

Villas

What they are: just 101 villas across the whole masterplan, including the Kadar Villas concept. Who they suit: buyers who want the rarest product in the community. With so few villas, availability moves quickly, so ask early if Mira Coral Bay villas are on your list.

Floor plans, sizes by collection and the latest payment plan are in the brochure. Completion is phased, with the first collections scheduled from late 2028.

Where Is Al Mairid?

Al Mairid is a coastal district on the northern side of Ras Al Khaimah city, beside the historic heart of the emirate, where the shoreline meets the Hajar mountains behind it. It is already home to the Hilton Ras Al Khaimah Resort and Spa, and Marjan is building it out as a waterfront leisure destination.

Life Inside Mira Coral Bay

The community is designed to work like a resort you live in. Alongside the homes are four five-star resorts, two branded beach clubs, a yacht club with marina, fine dining, a retail walk and a private stretch of beach reached from inside the community. Streets are planned car-free, with promenades linking the clusters to the water.

Mira Coral Bay Buyer Checklist

  • ◆ Decide which brand collection suits you, not just the unit size
  • ◆ Check which collections are open now and their completion dates
  • ◆ Ask for the service charge, including hotel-style services
  • ◆ Understand the rental programme options if you plan to let the home
  • ◆ Confirm the payment plan for your specific collection
  • ◆ Budget for Ras Al Khaimah registration fees on top of the price
  • ◆ Visit or video-tour Al Mairid so you know the area, not just the renders

Common Mistakes, and What to Do Instead

Treating all 14 brands as the same product

Instead: Compare collections side by side: design, size, price per square foot and completion date all differ.

Assuming it is in Dubai

Instead: Mira Coral Bay is in Ras Al Khaimah. Plan your commute and lifestyle around RAK, around 1 hour 15 minutes from Dubai.

Looking only at the entry price

Instead: Factor in service charges for a fully serviced branded home, and compare net rental returns rather than gross.

Waiting for the villas to come back

Instead: With only 101 villas in the masterplan, register interest early if a villa is what you want.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Mira Coral Bay?+
Mira Coral Bay is on the waterfront in Al Mairid, Ras Al Khaimah, around 10 minutes from Downtown Ras Al Khaimah and roughly 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes from Dubai. It is in Ras Al Khaimah, not Dubai.
Who is the developer of Mira Coral Bay?+
Mira Developments, in a joint venture with Marjan, the master developer of freehold property in Ras Al Khaimah and of Al Marjan Island.
What does multi-branded mean?+
Instead of one brand, 14 luxury brands each create and furnish their own building or cluster within the same masterplan, including Dolce&Gabbana Casa, Bentley Home, ETRO Home, Jacob & Co., Trussardi and John Richmond.
How much is an apartment at Mira Coral Bay?+
Prices start from around AED 550,000, with pricing varying by brand collection, size and view. Request the brochure for the current price list.
Are there villas at Mira Coral Bay?+
Yes. The masterplan includes 101 villas, including Mira's Kadar Villas concept, alongside townhouses and more than 800 apartments.
Are the homes furnished?+
Yes. Homes are delivered fully furnished and serviced by the relevant brand partner.
Can foreigners buy at Mira Coral Bay?+
Yes. Mira Coral Bay is freehold and open to international buyers.
When is completion?+
Completion is phased by collection, with the first collections scheduled from late 2028. Confirm the date for the specific collection you are considering.
Can I rent my Mira Coral Bay home out?+
The homes are furnished and set within a hospitality-led community, which suits rental. Ask about the rental options for your chosen collection before you buy.
