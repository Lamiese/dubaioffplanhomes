Golf Fields is Emaar Properties' newest launch within Emaar South, featuring 1, 2 and 3 bedroom apartments alongside 3 bedroom townhouses overlooking an 18-hole championship golf course. The launch adds two new golf-front buildings, GB03 and GB04, to the wider Emaar South masterplan, positioned directly on the fairway with sweeping views across the course. With prices starting from AED 1.26 million and handover scheduled for early 2030, Golf Fields brings Emaar's established design and delivery standards to one of Dubai's fastest growing corridors.

Why Golf Fields Is Generating Strong Early Interest

A trusted, established developer

Golf Fields is developed by Emaar Properties, the name behind Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina and Emaar Beachfront. Buyers investing in Golf Fields benefit from Emaar's track record of consistent delivery and long-term value across its master communities, a significant reassurance for a golf-front launch at this stage.

Genuine golf course-front positioning

Golf Fields sits directly on the fairway of Emaar South's 18-hole championship golf course, with GB03 and GB04 positioned to capture sweeping course views. Golf-front positioning within a freehold Emaar community at this stage of launch is a genuinely rare opportunity, and typically commands a premium once the course and surrounding buildings mature.

Resort scale amenities

Residents at Golf Fields will have access to resort-style infinity pools, fully equipped indoor and outdoor gyms, padel courts and landscaped parks, alongside the golf course itself and its clubhouse facilities. It's a comprehensive lifestyle offering built around an active, outdoor-focused way of living.

Al Maktoum Airport corridor momentum

Emaar South sits within the rapidly expanding Al Maktoum International Airport corridor, just 5 to 10 minutes from the airport and Expo City Dubai. As this corridor continues its infrastructure expansion, Emaar South and its surrounding phases, including Golf Point, Golf Acres and Golf Vale, are positioned for strong long-term investment fundamentals.
