How Dubai Off-Plan Payment Plans Work

Most off-plan developers in Dubai split the purchase price into a down payment, a series of construction-linked instalments, and a handover payment due when you receive the keys. Some developers also offer a post-handover plan, letting you spread part of the price over one to five years after moving in.

The exact split varies significantly by developer and project. A 60/40 plan is one of the most common structures, with 60% paid during construction and 40% on handover. A 1% monthly plan spreads payments in small, predictable monthly amounts throughout the construction period. Post-handover plans, such as 20/80, reduce the amount due before handover and extend a larger portion of the price over several years afterward, which can ease cash flow for investors.

This calculator lets you model any of these structures against a real property price, so you can see roughly what your monthly commitment would look like before speaking to a specific developer.
