RAW District 2 is a dynamic mixed-use development from Imtiaz Developments, positioned on Sheikh Zayed Road within Downtown Jebel Ali. The project combines fully furnished studio, 1, 2 and 3 bedroom apartments alongside premium office spaces and retail outlets, offering a rare combination of residential, commercial and retail investment within a single integrated address. Designed for modern urban living and business, RAW District 2 brings strong long-term value to one of Dubai's emerging growth corridors.

Why RAW District 2 Is Generating Strong Early Interest

Most Dubai off-plan launches are purely residential. RAW District 2 stands apart by combining apartments, office spaces and retail units within one integrated development, giving investors the flexibility to choose the property type that best matches their strategy, whether that's rental income from a residential unit, business space, or a retail outlet in a growing commercial corridor.

An accessible Sheikh Zayed Road address

With a starting price of AED 666,000, RAW District 2 offers one of the more accessible entry points into a Sheikh Zayed Road address currently available in Dubai. Sizes range from 850 to 3,200 sq ft across studio, 1, 2 and 3 bedroom apartments, plus office and retail units.

Flexible payment structures

Imtiaz Developments is offering buyer-friendly payment plans on RAW District 2, including a 50/50 structure and a post-handover 60/40 option, giving buyers more flexibility around when they commit the bulk of their capital relative to construction and handover.

Downtown Jebel Ali momentum

RAW District 2 sits within Downtown Jebel Ali, a strategic and fast-growing corridor along Sheikh Zayed Road (E11). The location offers seamless connectivity to major commercial hubs and lifestyle destinations, positioning residents and businesses within easy reach of the wider city while benefiting from the area's expanding infrastructure.
