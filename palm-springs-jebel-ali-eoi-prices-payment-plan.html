What Is the Palm Springs EOI?

An Expression of Interest is the step before a booking. It tells Nakheel which type of home you want, ahead of units being allocated. For Palm Springs, the EOI is prepared and submitted by a registered agent through Nakheel's broker portal, which means buyers cannot lodge one directly.

In practice, an EOI involves a signed EOI form, a copy of your passport (and Emirates ID for UAE residents) and an EOI deposit. Nakheel accepts manager's cheques, personal cheques, bank transfers and card payments. Once the deposit has cleared, the EOI status moves from submitted to paid.

The deposit amount, what happens if you do not proceed, and how allocation is prioritised are all points to confirm with your agent before you pay. Ask us and we will set them out for the home type you are considering.

Why Palm Springs, and Why Now

Dubai's population has passed 4 million and continues to grow, and family homes with gardens have been one of the tightest parts of the market. New villa and townhouse supply is concentrated in a handful of master communities, which is why first releases from major developers tend to draw heavy demand.

Palm Springs sits in Dubai's southern growth corridor, beside Sheikh Zayed Road and around 14 minutes from Palm Jebel Ali. At the briefing, Nakheel presented its case on three points: an early-entry price into a major new destination, larger plots with lower density than comparable prime communities, and the price growth already seen in nearby villa communities. We can share the comparison data presented on request.

Two protections apply to every off-plan purchase in Dubai. Your payments go into a project escrow account regulated by the Dubai Land Department and RERA, released to the developer against construction progress. And with every home here starting above AED 2 million, Palm Springs sits above the property threshold for the 10 year UAE Golden Visa, subject to the rules in force when you apply.

Aliso Townhouses: 3 and 4 Bedrooms

What they are

The Aliso collection is the entry point to Palm Springs: 3 bedroom townhouses over two floors and 4 bedroom townhouses in two formats, one over two floors and a larger corner layout over three floors with a family room and roof terrace. Each has a private garden, a maid's room and a two-car carport.

Location and amenities

The townhouse neighbourhoods sit on walkable streets that lead to Canyon Park, the waterway and garden landscape at the centre of the community, and to Thousand Palms Country Club, with its sand-beach pool, kids' pools, restaurants and event lawn.

Who it suits

Young families moving up from an apartment, and investors who want the lowest entry price into the Palm Jebel Ali corridor. Prices start at AED 3.8 million.

Rancho Villas: 4 and 5 Bedrooms

What they are

The Rancho villas are standalone homes over three floors, with space for a private pool, open-plan living that opens onto the garden, en-suite bedrooms and a roof terrace. The 5 bedroom adds a family living room and a ground floor guest suite. The villas are served by district cooling, which means quieter homes and no large cooling units on the roof.

Location and amenities

Rancho villa neighbourhoods are set around Canyon Park, within easy reach of Silver Spur Racquet Club, with courts, a gym, fitness studios and a lap pool.

Who it suits

Established families who want space and privacy, and buyers comparing coastal villa communities who would rather put the difference into a bigger plot. Villas start at AED 7.0 million.

The Palm Springs Payment Plan

Palm Springs uses a 60/40 plan. You pay 10% on booking, then 50% in seven stages during construction, starting in April 2027 and finishing in May 2029. The remaining 40% is due on handover, estimated for October 2030.

The 40% handover payment can be funded by a mortgage for eligible buyers. Dubai Land Department registration fees are payable on top of the purchase price. To see how the instalments fall against your own budget, use our payment plan calculator.

Your Palm Springs EOI Checklist

  • ◆ Decide on your home type and a realistic budget range before the EOI
  • ◆ Have a valid passport copy ready, plus Emirates ID if you live in the UAE
  • ◆ Confirm the EOI deposit amount and its terms with your agent
  • ◆ Choose your payment method, and if paying by transfer, keep the confirmation
  • ◆ Make sure your Application Reference Number appears on every payment document
  • ◆ Plan cash for the 10% booking payment and DLD registration fees
  • ◆ Check the estimated service charge, including the clubs and shared facilities
  • ◆ Line up mortgage pre-approval early if you plan to finance the handover payment

Common Mistakes, and What to Do Instead

Waiting for the public launch

Instead: Register now. EOIs are lodged before units are allocated, so buyers who wait for the public launch choose from what is left.

Comparing on headline price only

Instead: Compare price per square foot, plot size and position. Two homes of the same type can differ meaningfully on plot.

Paying the deposit without a reference

Instead: Wait for your Application Reference Number and quote it on every cheque or transfer, so the payment is matched to your EOI.

Forgetting the handover payment

Instead: Plan for the 40% due in 2030 from day one, whether from savings, a sale or a mortgage.

Confusing Palm Springs with Palm Jebel Ali

Instead: Palm Springs is a mainland community of townhouses and villas. Palm Jebel Ali is Nakheel's island project nearby, at a different price point.
