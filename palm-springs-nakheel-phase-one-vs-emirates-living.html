What Is Palm Springs by Nakheel Phase One?

Palm Springs is an upcoming master-planned community from Nakheel, the developer behind Palm Jumeirah and Dubai Islands, set on the mainland in the Jebel Ali area close to Palm Jebel Ali. It is planned as a low-rise, houses-only neighbourhood: townhouses and standalone villas arranged around landscaped parks, walkable pathways, recreational facilities and a central country club.

The development is planned in two phases. Phase one is the opening release and the one buyers will be choosing from first. Unit types, floor plans, prices and the payment plan are published by the developer at launch, and Nakheel confirmed Palm Springs pricing at its November 2026 broker briefing. Our full Palm Springs guide and project page carry the detail as it is released.

A brand-new, low-rise villa and townhouse community from one of Dubai's best-known master developers, in a part of the city that is changing fast.

Why Jebel Ali Is a Corridor to Watch

Jebel Ali sits in southwest Dubai on Sheikh Zayed Road, the main route towards Abu Dhabi, and it is one of the emirate's main growth corridors. Nakheel is building Palm Jebel Ali nearby, and Palm Springs gives buyers a Nakheel-built home on the mainland rather than on the island, which is a different product to the island homes.

A houses-only community in a mostly apartment market

Most new Dubai launches are apartment towers. Townhouse and villa communities with parks, a country club and low-rise streets are rarer in new supply, which is part of why Palm Springs is drawing attention from families.

Buyer protections on off-plan purchases

In Dubai, off-plan payments are paid into regulated escrow accounts and the project is registered with the Dubai Land Department, which is a core difference from buying elsewhere. Jebel Ali is also a designated freehold area, so non-UAE nationals can own outright.

Golden Visa position

Dubai property worth AED 2 million or more can qualify for the 10-year Golden Visa, subject to current rules. See our Golden Visa guide for how it works.

What Is Emirates Living?

Emirates Living is Emaar's group of established freehold villa communities between Sheikh Zayed Road and Al Khail Road in central Dubai. It is generally understood to include The Springs, The Meadows, The Lakes and Emirates Hills, and it is a mature, fully built resale market with no new off-plan launches. Homes have been occupied for more than 20 years, so lakes, trees, schools and shops are all already in place.

The Springs

The largest and most accessible community, with around 4,800 two to four bedroom townhouses across 15 clusters around lakes and parks. Market summaries in 2026 put a ready two bedroom home at around AED 3.5 million to AED 3.6 million and a three bedroom at around AED 5.5 million, with gross rental yields often quoted at about 4% to 5.5%.

The Meadows

Launched in 2002, The Meadows has larger detached villas of four to six bedrooms. Three bedroom homes average around AED 5 million and larger villas run from the high single-digit millions to well above AED 10 million depending on plot and cluster.

The Lakes

A smaller community of around 600 villas and townhouses on the Emirates Golf Club edge, with three to seven bedroom homes. Listing averages range from roughly AED 3.6 million for a three bedroom home to AED 7.8 million for five bedrooms.

Emirates Living is not one price. It runs from relatively accessible townhouses in The Springs to ultra-prime Emirates Hills mansions, so the fair comparison with Palm Springs townhouses and villas is The Springs, The Meadows and The Lakes.

Where Palm Springs looks stronger

If the indicated prices hold, a new three bedroom Palm Springs townhouse would cost noticeably less than a ready three bedroom home in The Springs, and you would get a brand-new home with a modern layout and a payment plan that spreads the cost. Buyers also avoid renovation costs that older homes can need.

Where Emirates Living looks stronger

You can see, walk through and move into what you are buying today. Rental income starts immediately, the community is proven and central, and schools, retail and golf are on the doorstep. There is no handover wait, no construction risk and no delivery date to rely on.

The honest trade-off

Palm Springs offers a lower likely entry price and a newer home in exchange for a long wait, an unfinished neighbourhood and a location further from central Dubai. Emirates Living offers certainty and income now at a higher price per home. Neither is better in the abstract. They suit different buyers.

Palm Springs Phase One FAQs

What is Palm Springs by Nakheel phase one?+
Palm Springs is an upcoming Nakheel community of townhouses and standalone villas in the Jebel Ali area of Dubai, planned in two phases. Phase one is the first release Nakheel will put on sale. Unit types, floor plans, prices and the payment plan for phase one have not been officially published yet.
When does Palm Springs phase one launch?+
Brokers expect the launch in November 2026, and some name a date in the second half of the month, but Nakheel has not confirmed it. Treat any date as provisional until Nakheel announces it. Registering interest means you are told when details are released.
What does registering interest cost, and does it commit me to buy?+
Registering your interest with us is free and does not commit you to buy. It puts you on the list to receive the brochure, prices, floor plans and payment plan as soon as they are released. Registration does not reserve or guarantee a unit. Units are allocated by Nakheel at launch, and a booking deposit is only paid if you choose to reserve.
How much will Palm Springs cost compared with Emirates Living?+
Confirmed prices put 3 and 4 bedroom Palm Springs townhouses from AED 3.8 million, with some reports as low as AED 3.3 million, and standalone villas from AED 7.0 million to AED 8.9 million. Ready townhouses and villas in Emirates Living are typically priced from the mid single-digit millions for a three bedroom home, rising well above that in The Meadows, The Lakes and Emirates Hills. Palm Springs figures are unconfirmed and the homes are not like for like, so compare confirmed prices and sizes once released.
Is Palm Springs the same as The Springs in Emirates Living?+
No. The Springs is an established Emaar community inside Emirates Living, built more than 20 years ago. Palm Springs is a separate, brand-new Nakheel community in Jebel Ali that has no connection beyond a similar name. It is also unrelated to the older Palm Springs name once used for a different project on Palm Jebel Ali.
Which is better for rental income, Palm Springs or Emirates Living?+
Emirates Living is a mature rental market where homes are ready to let today, with gross yields commonly quoted at roughly 4% to 5.5% depending on community and home type. Palm Springs cannot earn rent until handover, which early information suggests is several years away, and its rental performance is untested. Emirates Living suits buyers who want income now; Palm Springs suits buyers who accept a long wait for a new home.
How does the Palm Springs payment plan compare with buying in Emirates Living?+
Palm Springs has a confirmed 60/40 plan: 10% on booking, 50% during construction and 40% on handover. Emirates Living homes are resale properties, so you normally pay the full price at transfer, funded by cash or a mortgage. The Palm Springs plan spreads the cost over the build period, but it is not confirmed until Nakheel publishes it.
Do Palm Springs and Emirates Living qualify for the UAE Golden Visa?+
Dubai property worth AED 2 million or more can qualify an owner for the 10-year Golden Visa, subject to the authorities' current rules and your own eligibility. Palm Springs townhouses at the indicated prices and most Emirates Living homes sit above that threshold, but always confirm the current criteria before relying on it.
Can foreigners buy at Palm Springs or in Emirates Living?+
Yes. Jebel Ali is a designated freehold area where non-UAE nationals can own property, and Emirates Living is a freehold Emaar community open to foreign buyers. Final ownership terms for Palm Springs will be confirmed at launch.
