About Hado by Beyond

Hado by Beyond is a wellbeing-focused, Japanese-inspired residential development within the Siora community on Dubai Islands. Comprising three towers rising 21 residential levels above a ground floor and basement, the project is positioned along North Beach on Island B, with layouts designed around natural light, balance and unobstructed coastal views. The name Hado draws on Japanese concepts of energy, frequency and intention, principles that run through the architecture, interior design and amenity offering.

Each tower is shaped around clean geometry, horizontal tiering and corner glazing, with 3.2-metre floor-to-ceiling heights in all living areas and interiors featuring warm oak tones, stone surfaces and curved joinery. The development offers 1 to 4-bedroom apartments alongside premium duplex configurations, all within a coastal masterplan that includes promenades, parks, marine experiences and premium hotels. Hado is developed by Beyond Developments, a contemporary brand under Omniyat with an active portfolio across Dubai's prime coastal districts.

Hado sits within Siora on Dubai Islands' North Beach, one of the most connected and amenity-rich positions within the wider masterplan. With a 50/50 payment plan, Q3 2029 handover and starting prices from AED 2.2M, the project offers a compelling entry point into Dubai Islands' premium waterfront segment with the backing of Omniyat behind it.

About Beyond Developments

Beyond Developments is a contemporary development brand created under Omniyat, one of Dubai's most respected luxury real estate groups. Beyond is designed to deliver modern, design-led residential communities focused on balance, proportion and natural light. The brand's active portfolio includes Sensia and Orise in Dubai Maritime City and Passo on Palm Jumeirah's East Crescent. Hado extends Beyond's approach into Dubai Islands, applying the same architectural discipline and lifestyle-led planning that defines the broader portfolio. With Omniyat's backing and a growing residential pipeline, Beyond is positioning itself as a key developer across Dubai's premium coastal districts.
