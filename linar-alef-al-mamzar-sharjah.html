Linar sits on Al Mamzar Beach in Sharjah, directly on the border with Dubai and within easy reach of both emirates. Developed by Alef Group, Linar is a waterfront community of 5 residential towers and a future commercial tower, set across 5.5 hectares on the east edge of the beach. The project brings coastal living, integrated retail and urban convenience into one address, with a public plaza, amphitheatre, pocket gardens and a dedicated Level 6 amenity deck giving residents two distinct lifestyle layers within the same community.

Why Linar Is Generating Strong Early Interest

A rare beachfront address at an accessible price

With entry prices from AED 849,000 for a 1 bedroom apartment, Linar offers direct waterfront positioning with uninterrupted Arabian Gulf views at a price point well below comparable beachfront addresses in Dubai. A limited collection of just 4 four-bedroom penthouses at 319.01 sqm is available from AED 4.99M for buyers seeking a larger, more exclusive residence.

Backed by an established destination developer

Alef Group is one of Sharjah's leading destination developers, known for large-scale, master-planned communities. Linar is the latest addition to Alef's portfolio, bringing the same long-term asset confidence to a genuinely coastal address.

Two layers of amenity, one address

At the centre of the community sits a public plaza animated by interactive water features, an amphitheatre, pocket gardens and landscaped walkways. A dedicated Level 6 amenity deck adds lap and family pools, kids' pools, male and female gyms, yoga zones, a teen recreation centre and majlis seating, giving residents both a public, community-facing layer and a private, resident-only layer within the same development.

Direct connectivity into Dubai

Linar sits along Corniche and Al Taawun Street, with direct access to Al Wuheida Road and Al Ittihad Road, Sharjah's two main connectors into Dubai. The Dubai border is just 4 minutes away via Al Ittihad Road, and Dubai International Airport is reachable in 15 minutes, making Linar a realistic option for buyers and tenants who work in Dubai but want a beachfront address at a Sharjah price point.

Built with future-resilient materials

Linar is being built with a focus on sustainability credentials, including rubberized asphalt roads, lower-carbon concrete and integrated photovoltaic panels, supporting long-term asset value as environmental standards continue to tighten across the region.

Cross-border opportunity: Linar is located in Sharjah rather than Dubai, and is featured here because of its direct connectivity into Dubai and the coastal value it offers relative to comparable Dubai waterfront addresses. Buyers should be aware this is a Sharjah freehold community rather than a Dubai one when evaluating the investment. Living at Al Mamzar Beach: What to Expect

Connectivity

The community also sits 20 minutes from Sharjah International Airport, giving residents practical access to both emirates' air travel infrastructure.
