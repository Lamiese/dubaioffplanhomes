About Bashayer

Bashayer is a waterfront community on Hudayriyat Island, developed by Modon Properties. Drawing on the look and feel of European coastal villages, the development sits directly on the water along Abu Dhabi's fast-growing island district, best known for its beaches, cycling trails, and the Surf Abu Dhabi wave pool.

The community is split into two distinct offerings: a gated enclave of low-rise villas, and a waterfront residential building of apartments and penthouses, both built around shared resort-style amenities and direct access to the marina promenade.

Bashayer Villas

A gated collection of 4 and 5-bedroom villas ranging from 365 to 590 sqm, set within a quiet, low-density waterfront enclave. Each villa is designed with generous interior space, private outdoor areas, and close proximity to the water's edge.

Bashayer Residences

A collection of 1 to 3-bedroom apartments and 4-bedroom penthouses within a single waterfront building, featuring floor-to-ceiling glazing, private balconies, and uninterrupted views across the Abu Dhabi skyline and coastline.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the expected handover date for Bashayer?

Villas are scheduled for handover in Q1 2029, while residences are expected to hand over in Q1 2030.

Are Bashayer homeowners eligible for the UAE Golden Visa?

Yes. Starting prices for both apartments and villas at Bashayer exceed the AED 2 million minimum threshold required to apply for the 10-year Golden Visa.

What is the starting price at Bashayer?

One-bedroom apartments start from AED 2.35 million, and four to five-bedroom villas start from AED 7.2 million. A 50/50 payment plan applies, with 10% due on booking, 40% during construction, and 50% on handover.

Where exactly is Bashayer located?

Bashayer is located on Hudayriyat Island in Abu Dhabi, close to Bashayer Marina and the wider Hudayriyat Island master community, with direct access to the island's beaches and leisure trails.
