About Serra at Ghaf Woods

Serra is the first residential cluster to launch within Ghaf Woods, Majid Al Futtaim's landmark master community in Dubai. Set within a genuine 7 million square foot forest of indigenous Ghaf trees, Serra offers 1, 2 and 3-bedroom apartments designed around the concept of biophilic living — where nature is not a backdrop but an integral part of the home itself. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame uninterrupted views into the forest canopy, natural materials run throughout the interiors, and the sound and light of the forest permeate every residence.

Serra is the first of 11 planned clusters within the Ghaf Woods masterplan. As the inaugural phase, it offers the most competitive pricing within the community, with subsequent clusters expected to launch at progressively higher price points. Ghaf Woods is positioned next to Global Village along Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road, placing it within easy reach of Dubai's key destinations while offering a living environment found nowhere else in the city.

Ghaf Woods is the first community in Dubai built within and around a forest of indigenous Ghaf trees, a species protected by UAE law. The development is designed around the principle of biophilic living and represents a genuine departure from conventional Dubai residential development. Serra, as the first cluster to launch, offers the most accessible entry point into this unique community.

About Majid Al Futtaim

Majid Al Futtaim is one of the Middle East's most prominent conglomerates, established in 1992 and operating across 17 international markets. The company manages 29 shopping malls, 13 hotels and multiple mixed-use communities, including the Mall of the Emirates and the City Centre mall network. In residential development, MAF's most celebrated project is Tilal Al Ghaf, a premium mixed-use community built around a lagoon in Dubai, which has consistently ranked among the most sought-after addresses in the city. Ghaf Woods represents MAF's next major residential chapter and the most ambitious community it has undertaken to date.
