The Meriva Collection is a landmark beachfront launch from Ellington Properties, arriving on Island B of Dubai Islands. Phase 1, known as Meriva Sunset, introduces six architecturally distinct buildings comprising 903 residences, all set within a private pedestrianised enclave defined by flowing waterways and curated landscapes. Designed as a low-density coastal retreat, the collection brings Ellington's design led approach together with an on-site hospitality element, offering residents a rare blend of residential privacy and resort style service on one of Dubai's newest island destinations.

Why The Meriva Collection Is Generating Strong Early Interest

A trusted, design led developer

Ellington Properties has built a strong reputation across Dubai for boutique, detail focused residential buildings that balance style, functionality and comfort. The Meriva Collection continues that philosophy, bringing considered materials, elegant architecture and a genuine sense of place to Island B of Dubai Islands. For buyers who value design quality over generic finishes, Ellington's track record carries real weight.

A genuine beachfront address with hospitality integration

The Meriva Collection sits directly on the beach, part of a private pedestrianised enclave with flowing waterways and curated landscapes. What sets the development apart is the integration of hospitality within a residential environment, with an on-site hotel component operated by a reputable hospitality operator. This resort style approach, combined with genuine beachfront positioning within a freehold community at this stage of launch, is rare in Dubai's current off plan market.

Resort scale amenities

The amenities at The Meriva Collection go well beyond a standard pool and gym. Residents will have access to infinity pools, a fitness pavilion and Pilates studio, lagoon features, an indoor pool and spa, kids' play zones, a private cinema and curated social spaces throughout the pedestrianised enclave. It is one of the most comprehensive amenity offerings among recent Dubai Islands launches.

Dubai Islands momentum

Dubai Islands is one of Dubai's most significant new coastal developments, aligned with the Dubai 2040 Urban Master Plan and designed to create sustainable, interconnected communities. With direct connectivity to Sheikh Zayed Road, the masterplan is expected to deliver strong long term capital growth as infrastructure and amenities continue to mature across its five islands.
