Soma Residences is a new wellness led launch by Origami Development on Dubai Islands. The name draws on the Greek word for body, the physical form that experiences and inhabits the world, reflecting a design approach where light, air and the sensory quality of materials are treated as a primary consideration rather than an afterthought. The low-rise architecture is defined by a sculptural silhouette in sun-bleached stone, sand-toned surfaces and weathered timber, with generous balconies across every residence. With 1, 2 and 3 bedroom apartments alongside 3 bedroom duplexes, Soma Residences offers a genuinely different lifestyle proposition among current Dubai Islands launches.

Why Soma Residences Is Generating Strong Early Interest

A wellness led design philosophy

Soma Residences is built around a holistic approach to wellbeing that integrates the physical and sensory experience of everyday living. The architecture uses sun-bleached stone, sand-toned surfaces and weathered timber to create a calm, grounded atmosphere, with generous balconies on every residence designed to draw natural light and sea air into daily life. For buyers who want more than a standard apartment finish, this design led approach sets Soma Residences apart.

An accessible entry point on Dubai Islands

With an EOI phase starting price of AED 1.67 million, Soma Residences offers one of the more accessible entry points into Dubai Islands' current supply. Apartments range from 848 to 1,607 sq ft, with 3 bedroom duplexes at 2,355 sq ft for buyers wanting larger, more villa like layouts within a low-rise residential setting.

Resort scale amenities

Residents will have access to a rooftop terrace with padel court, wellness focused amenities and communal gardens designed around a relaxed, wellness led lifestyle. The low-rise scale of the development supports a genuinely community focused environment rather than a high density tower experience.

Dubai Islands momentum

Dubai Islands is one of Dubai's most significant new coastal developments, comprising five interconnected man-made islands designed to blend residential, commercial and leisure spaces. Positioned along the coast with well developed infrastructure connecting to the mainland, the masterplan places residents within easy reach of Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina and Palm Jumeirah while offering the tranquility of island living.
